# Tenant Administration

The topic in this section applies to SAP Datasphere tenants provisioned either in a SAP Business Data Cloud formation or in SAP Business Technology Platform.

After your SAP Datasphere tenant has been provisioned, you can configure the resources allocated to it based on your business and workload requirements. Tenant sizing is performed in SAP Datasphere and is available regardless of whether the tenant was provisioned through a SAP Business Data Cloud or directly in SAP Business Technology Platform.

The topic in this section describes how to configure the size of your SAP Datasphere tenant, including storage, memory, compute resources, and other available capacity options.
