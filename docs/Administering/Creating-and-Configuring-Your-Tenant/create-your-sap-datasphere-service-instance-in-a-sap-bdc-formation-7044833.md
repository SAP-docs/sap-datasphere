# Create Your SAP Datasphere Service Instance in a SAP BDC Formation

If you subscribe to SAP Datasphere through SAP Business Data Cloud \(SAP BDC\), you must provision your SAP Datasphere tenant through SAP for Me as part of the SAP BDC provisioning process.

For information about provisioning SAP Datasphere in SAP BDC, see [Provisioning SAP Datasphere](https://help.sap.com/docs/business-data-cloud/administering-sap-business-data-cloud/provisioning-sap-datasphere) in the *Administering SAP Business Data Cloud* guide.

After provisioning is complete, you can perform tenant administration tasks in SAP Datasphere as described in this guide.

> ### Note:  
> This information is for creating an SAP Datasphere tenant in SAP BDC. If your access is in SAP BTP, you must follow the procedure in [Create Your SAP Datasphere Service Instance in SAP BTP](create-your-sap-datasphere-service-instance-in-sap-btp-54288aa.md).
