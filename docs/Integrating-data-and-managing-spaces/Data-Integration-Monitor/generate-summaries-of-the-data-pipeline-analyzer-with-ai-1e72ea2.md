# Generate Summaries of the Data Pipeline Analyzer With AI

Leverage AI to gain deeper insights into your data pipeline for Views, Tables, and Analytic Models. You can generate a Business Summary to quickly understand how your pipeline is structured, or create an Errors and Impacts Summary to identify issues and pinpoint any blocked data movements.



## Prerequisites

To use SAP Business AI features to generate summaries for your data pipeline, you must enable in your tenant the *AI-Assisted Data Monitoring - AI-Enhanced Data Pipeline Analyzer Summary* option under *System* \> *Configuration* \> *AI Services* \(see [Enable SAP Business AI for SAP Datasphere](https://help.sap.com/viewer/9f804b8efa8043539289f42f372c4862/STABI/en-US/1b3fe45f38df4db1a9cda97a5a7bcdaf.html "SAP Business AI is a fully managed service by SAP that allows you to integrate artificial intelligence (AI) models in different business solutions. SAP Business AI provides a simple and easy-to-use API with various endpoints that you can use in your solution for different tasks such as text generation, summarization, language translation, creative content development. Enable SAP Business AI and Joule for SAP Datasphere to integrate AI content recommendations. This will simplify access to information, automate business processes, and generate AI content recommendations in various areas of SAP Datasphere.") :arrow_upper_right:\) and you must also have a global role that grants you the following privilege:

-   **Data Warehouse AI Consumption \(–--–E--\) - To use SAP Business AI features**

-   **Data Warehouse Data Integration** \(-**R**------\) or **Data Warehouse Data Builder** \(-R------\) To start the **Data Pipeline Analyzer**

**The DW Integrator** or **The DW Modeler** role templates coupled with the **DW AI Consumer** global role, for example, grant these privileges. ## Generate the Summaries

To start the *AI-Enhanced Data Pipeline Analyzer Summary* feature,

1.  Open the *Data Pipeline Analyzer*. For more information, see [Data Pipeline Analyzer](data-pipeline-analyzer-54fd00d.md).
2.  Click the *Details* button in the top right corner

The feature opens in 2 tabs:

-   Description:

    This tab provides a business summary of your data pipeline, including its purpose, key transformations, and details about data sources and targets. You can create a summary manually by selecting *Add Summary*, or use *Generate Summary* to let AI create one for you.

    > ### Note:  
    > Once a business summary is created \(either by yourself or by AI\), you can edit it at any time using the *Edit* button. After saving, the same summary will be displayed each time you open it.

-   Status:

    In this tab, you can use AI to generate an *Errors and Impacts Summary*. This helps you quickly identify errors and detect any blocked data movements.

    > ### Note:  
    > This summary is read-only and can only be generated by AI. It cannot be persisted because it may change at any time.


You can copy either summary using the *Copy* button or create a new version using *Regenerate*.
